# Provider logo sources

`ProviderLogo.tsx` embeds local monochrome SVG geometry. No runtime downloads.
Logos remain trademarks of their respective owners; their use identifies the
provider and does not imply endorsement.

- Anthropic, Google Gemini and ElevenLabs: Simple Icons, CC0-1.0.
  https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/blob/develop/LICENSE.md
  Sources: `icons/anthropic.svg`, `icons/googlegemini.svg`,
  `icons/elevenlabs.svg` in https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons.
- OpenAI: https://github.com/pheralb/svgl/blob/main/static/library/openai.svg.
  SVGL collection: MIT, https://github.com/pheralb/svgl/blob/main/LICENSE.
  Original path preserved; viewBox trimmed to remove surrounding whitespace.

All marks use `currentColor` to support light and dark backgrounds.
