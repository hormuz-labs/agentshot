# Names and logos

AgentShot's code is open source. Its name and its artwork are not part of that: they say who made a thing, and that
only works if they mean one maker.

This page says what you may do with them. It is a plain-language policy, not legal advice.

## What is covered

- The names **AgentShot** and, as a name for this software, **Manul**.
- The name **Hormuz Labs**.
- The AgentShot icon and logo (`build/icon.*`, `build/icons/`) and the manul mascot, in the app and on the website.

Hormuz Labs claims these as its trademarks. The [licence](LICENSE) that covers the code grants no right to use them,
as section 7(e) of the GNU General Public License v3 allows a licensor to say.

## What you may do without asking

- **Say what your work is built on.** "Based on AgentShot", "a fork of AgentShot" and "works with AgentShot" are fine,
  as long as it is true and it is clear that your work is not AgentShot and is not made by Hormuz Labs.
- **Write and talk about AgentShot** by name: reviews, tutorials, videos, comparisons, talks.
- **Pass on the official builds unchanged**, under their own name.
- **Package AgentShot for a system** (a distribution's repository, for example) under its name, if what you ship is
  built from this repository's releases with only the changes that system needs to run it.

## What you may not do

- **Give a changed version these names.** If you distribute a fork or a modified build, give it a name of its own
  that could not be taken for AgentShot or Manul, and replace the icon, the logo and the mascot.
- **Use the names or artwork for a product, a service, a company, a domain, an app store listing or a social
  account** in a way that suggests it is AgentShot or comes from Hormuz Labs.
- **Suggest that Hormuz Labs endorses or supports** your work, unless it has said so.
- **Sell goods carrying the names or the artwork.**

## If you fork

The code is yours to change and to share under its licence. Before you distribute what you made:

1. Change the name everywhere a person would see it: the window title, the installer, the package name, the website.
2. Replace the icon, the logo and the mascot with your own.
3. Keep the copyright and licence notices in [NOTICE](NOTICE), as the licence requires. Saying where the code came
   from is welcome; using its name as yours is not.
